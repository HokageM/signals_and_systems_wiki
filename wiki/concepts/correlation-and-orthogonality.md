# Orthogonal systems and correlation

Source: [Lecture 1, pp. 13–15](../../raw/signals_1.pdf).

## Inner product

$$\langle x(t), y(t)\rangle = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x^*(t)\,y(t)\,dt
\qquad\qquad
\langle x[k], y[k]\rangle = \sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty} x^*[k]\,y[k]$$

Two signals are **orthogonal** if $\langle x(t), y(t)\rangle = 0$ (p. 13).
The energy of a signal can be written as $E_x = \langle x(t), x(t)\rangle$.

## Cross- and autocorrelation (energy signals)

$$r_{xy}^{E}(\tau) = \langle x(t), y(t+\tau)\rangle = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x^*(t)\,y(t+\tau)\,dt$$

$$r_{xx}^{E}(\tau) = \langle x(t), x(t+\tau)\rangle = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x^*(t)\,x(t+\tau)\,dt,
\qquad E_x = r_{xx}^{E}(0)$$

(p. 13–14).

## Cross- and autocorrelation (power signals)

$$r_{xy}^{P}(\tau) = \lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{1}{2T}\int_{-T}^{T} x^*(t)\,y(t+\tau)\,dt
\qquad\qquad
r_{xy}^{P}(\tau) = \frac{1}{T}\int_{t_0}^{t_0+T} x^*(t)\,y(t+\tau)\,dt \text{ (periodic case)}$$

with $P_x = r_{xx}^{P}(0)$ (p. 14).

## Properties of correlation

- $r_{yx}(\tau) = r_{xy}^{*}(-\tau)$, and discretely $r_{yx}[k] = r_{xy}^{*}[-k]$.
- $r_{xx}(\tau) = r_{xx}^{*}(-\tau)$ — the autocorrelation function is
  **conjugate-even** (see [Even/odd decomposition](even-odd-decomposition.md)).
- $r_{xx}(0) \ge |r_{xx}(\tau)|$ for all $\tau$ — the autocorrelation attains
  its maximum magnitude at zero lag (p. 15).

## Worked example: discrete autocorrelation

For $x[k] = [1,1,1,-1,-1,1,-1]$ (p. 16):

$$r_{xx}^{E}[k] = \begin{cases} 7, & k = 0 \\ -1, & |k| \in \{2,4,6\} \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
