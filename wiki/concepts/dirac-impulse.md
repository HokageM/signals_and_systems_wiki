# Dirac impulse

Source: [Lecture 1, p. 5](../../raw/signals_1.pdf).

## Definition

$$\delta(t) = \begin{cases} \neq 0, & t = 0 \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases},
\qquad \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \delta(t)\,dt = 1$$

Scaling the area: $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} a\,\delta(t)\,dt = a$.

## Sifting property

$$x(t)\,\delta(t - t_0) = x(t_0)\,\delta(t - t_0)$$

## Scaling of the argument

$$\delta(at) = \frac{1}{|a|}\,\delta(t)$$

## Dirac impulse comb

$$\mathrm{III}_T(t) = \sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty} \delta(t - kT)$$

a periodic train of unit impulses spaced by `T`, used later for sampling
(see [Elementary signals](elementary-signals.md) for a plot).
